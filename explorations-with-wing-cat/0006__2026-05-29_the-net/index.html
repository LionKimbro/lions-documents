A report on the Memory Intake System

Catchbasket

A lightweight capture inbox for the Internet: URLs, images, tweets, downloads, and other artifacts that Lion noticed before knowing exactly what they should become.

Created by Codex. Seeded from Lion's recorded ChatGPT/Wing-Cat conversation: chatgpt.com/c/6a19f510-a2f0-83e8-8370-ec7cedd793ae.

Central thesis

A pre-archive attentional buffer

Catchbasket is not primarily a bookmark manager, a notes app, or a final archive. It is the small holding area between noticing and deciding.

The design goal is simple: capture now, tag enough now, decide later. The act being preserved is not merely the URL or file. It is the fact that Lion noticed something in the stream and wanted that attention to survive.

The primary object is: "Lion noticed this."
