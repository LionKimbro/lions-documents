Architecture

Conversation as an event ecology

The system is best understood as a shared conversational workspace. Perception, memory, reasoning, and speaking authority are separated so the AI can think without blurting.

Internal Vocabulary

Events, not just messages

Event Meaning Example
PERCEPT Something noticed in the world. Speaker S1 began talking; volume rose; laughter occurred.
WONDER A private question raised by the surface mind. Is this question directed at the AI or at another person?
MUSING A possible interpretation, answer, or contribution. This resembles blackboard architecture plus turn-taking.
INTENTION A selected communicative move. Offer a brief answer when there is a pause.
SUPPRESSION A deliberate choice not to speak. The thought is correct but the humans are talking to each other.
DEFERMENT A choice to hold a thought for later. Save this as a note; mention it if the topic returns.

Voice Identity

What was said is not the same as who said it

Speech recognition

Turns audio into words. It answers: what sentence was spoken?

Speaker diarization

Segments the audio by voice. It answers: when did Speaker A, Speaker B, and Speaker C speak?

Voice recognition

Matches a voice to a known person or enrolled profile. It answers: is this Lion, Robert, or someone unknown?

A socially aware agent needs at least diarization, and eventually some form of consent-based voice recognition. Without that layer, it can transcribe words but cannot reliably know whose words they are, which makes turn-taking, memory use, and social standing much weaker.

These recognition technologies exist today in separate systems and APIs. The architectural challenge is turning them into participation: knowing whether the AI was addressed, whether it should remember the moment, and whether it has standing to speak.

Social Standing

The right to speak is computed

High standing

Direct address, explicit question, safety issue, requested explanation, or a long pause after a question.

Low standing

Two humans talking to each other, brief thinking silence, vulnerable moment, topic already moved on, or uncertain addressee.

Conditional standing

Useful thought with social risk. The system may ask permission, backchannel lightly, or save the idea for later.

Essential Addition

The social contract is part of the system

Ambient conversational AI should make its listening state, memory behavior, and participation mode legible. The user should know whether it is quiet, active, meeting-aware, private, logging, or allowed to speak without being directly addressed.

Speaker identity should begin as declared context and consent-based enrollment, not surprise biometric recognition. Manual labels, visible logs, voice profiles, consent controls, and session boundaries are not afterthoughts; they are what make the intelligence socially trustworthy.
